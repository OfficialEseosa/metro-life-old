# Light and image

The city uses metre units. One Canvas lives for the whole session: changing life, city or taking a trip never remounts it (the ride card covers the hidden city and the frame loop stops). The frame loop renders every animation frame while a life is played, on demand on the title screen, and not at all while the tab is hidden or a trip is under way. The canvas starts at the device pixel ratio clamped to 1-2.

**Time of day.** `lighting.ts` interpolates a palette between keyframes by game minute (night, dawn 06:00, morning 08:30, day 12:00-15:00, golden 17:30, dusk 19:15, night from 20:30): sun elevation, azimuth, colour and intensity; hemisphere sky/ground colours and intensity; sky top/bottom; fog; environment intensity; exposure; grade saturation, vignette and split-tone tints; shadow intensity; ground albedo. Window, lamp and headlight emission share one `lights` uniform that ramps on between 18:30 and 20:00 and off between 06:00 and 07:00. Scene code reads the palette every frame; React only re-renders when day turns to night (HUD theme, audio). The Day/Night preview button pins 12:30 or 22:30.

**Outdoor rig.** A gradient sky dome with a sun disc and halo and stars that fade in after dusk. A PMREM environment built from the same sky plus a ground disc, rebuilt only when the palette moves noticeably (a handful of times per game day). Hemisphere fill, one shadowed sun or moon, and a weak warm camera-side fill at night so faces stay readable. Linear fog in the horizon colour starts at 0.8x and ends at 3x the camera-to-target distance.

**Shadows.** PCF (`shadows="percentage"`). Within 60 m of the target the sun's shadow camera is a 52 m box centred just ahead of the player, snapped to the shadow texel grid and re-rendered every frame (every third on Low), so people, cars and swaying trees cast live shadows. Further out it covers the city (224 m) and re-renders only when the quantised sun direction changes. The PCF kernel radius is 2 near and 1.5 far; the normal offset is texel size x (radius + 0.6), which keeps lit surfaces from self-shadowing into a grey wash. Below 12 degrees the shadow caster stays at 12 degrees.

**Post.** Beauty, N8AO, SMAA, then a quarter-resolution bloom whose combine pass grades (saturation, contrast 1.06 around 0.18, violet shadow / warm highlight split toning, vignette) before Neutral tone mapping and output conversion. AO radius follows the camera distance (0.5-4 m), uses a violet-brown colour and is stronger indoors; phones and small screens compute it at half resolution. Bloom threshold and strength move from 1.6 / 0.10 by day to 1.05 / 0.22 after dark, so lit windows halo while skin and lamp pools stay below the threshold. Low quality omits AO. Sustained slow play downgrades to Low for the session only and recovers when frames are fast again; only the Settings choice is stored.

**Surfaces.** Grass is a world-space three-octave value-noise tint (the fine octave fades with distance); asphalt gets grain and darker wheel tracks, paving a 1.5 m tile grid with per-tile jitter. Trees are instanced, built in code from five clumps with normals pointing out of the crown, coloured from a darker base to a lighter top in five tints, swaying gently, split into 64 m tiles so the camera and the near shadow box skip far ones.

Primary implementation references: [N8AO usage and performance](https://github.com/N8python/n8ao), [Three SMAAPass](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/jsm/postprocessing/SMAAPass.js). N8AO 2.0.1 has no complete dispose override, so its owned render targets, quads and materials are explicitly released when size or quality changes.

## Rooms and staged activities
The original character GLBs are unchanged. CC0 Quaternius Universal Animation Libraries 1 and 2 are retargeted at 24 Hz onto both shipped 62-bone bodies by scripts/retarget-activities.mjs. Compressed character rest transforms are recovered from inverse bind matrices in the production Three loader. Retargeted Sit, Lie, Sleep, Type, Eat, Dance, Cheer, Jog and Talk use the existing single AnimationMixer and 0.2-second cross-fade. All ten wardrobe rigs share compatible bind transforms (maximum measured matrix difference 0.0000155).

shared/interiors.json owns city-specific furniture, walk-up stations, collision boxes and hip/facing slots. The server validates the approach and reserves a free slot, sends activitySlot in sim/presence/position frames and clears it at completion. The visual character walks into the slot without moving the authoritative approach position. Indoor actions last six seconds, leaving time for staging before the server settles money and needs. Park posture has a separate /api/posture endpoint, validates bench/proximity/occupancy, grants no money or XP and clears when walking resumes.

All four 3 m walls are built. Settings offers Walls Up, Cutaway (camera-facing upper walls hidden) and Walls Down (0.25 m remaining wall). Metre-scale chairs, desks, beds and tables follow the shared layout. Sleep fits under a raised blanket; the shower has frosted glass. Owned tiling floor and wallpaper textures, skirting, ceiling lamps, plants, shelves and city-specific clutter add room detail. Static furniture/floor contact is baked once; geometry, textures and wall resources are released when a room changes.

Runtime geometry also smooths coincident cloth normals (cached GLBs stay unchanged); each room has a supported ceiling diffuser and a darkened surrounding garden.
