# Light and image

The city keeps metre units and a full-resolution DPR of 1 in both Cozy and Low modes. Settings stores the quality choice when browser storage is available; blocked storage falls back safely to Cozy. Two consecutive three-second active-motion windows below 50 FPS switch automatically to Low; idle demand rendering and its intentional 30 Hz cadence are excluded.

The pipeline renders the world once, then applies N8AO (half-resolution, Performance preset, 0.65 m radius, bilinear upsampling), SMAA, gentle bloom (0.1 strength, 1.6 threshold, one quarter-size separable blur), a warm grade with 1.17 saturation, 1.10 contrast and a subtle 10% edge vignette combined with bloom and AgX output. Low omits N8AO. AgX and output colour conversion occur once in the final bloom/grade quad, using Three’s standard tone-mapping and colour-space chunks. This removes a separate full-resolution output pass. N8AO automatic transparency detection and transparency rerenders are disabled; transparent water/glass and light decals do not write scene depth.

GPU timer queries cover the pixel passes after the beauty render, including AO, AA, bloom, grade and output. An unsupported timer reports null. Draw/triangle metrics include all passes and refreshed shadows: automatic renderer info reset is disabled and one explicit reset runs before each complete frame. Idle scenes still use demand rendering.

Rooms add a warm hemisphere (0.5, 0.25 in the club), a camera-side warm key with a 16 m shadow region and 1024 map, and the procedural Three RoomEnvironment at 0.4 (0.22 in the club). Outdoor sun is excluded from interiors; a small global fill remains, reduced further in clubs. The club keeps a warm face key with pink and blue stage rim lights. The room shadow refreshes on position/activity changes, with moving activities limited to about15 updates per second. Imported building faces also receive a one-time BVH ray bake into owned vertex colours. Static furniture contacts are baked into vertex colours with six local hemisphere samples and segmented floors; Low retains these contacts. Lamps, stove and screens use local unshadowed lights; a club has three point lights, other rooms at most two. Glowing shades/windows are distinct surfaces. The private room lot has hedges and warm time-matched fog that begins beyond the room.

Night uses a dusk gradient and cooler moonlight. Neutral dark ground separates the city from the dusk gradient. Street lights get an instanced warm 10 m soft light-pool decal. The sedan has instanced front/rear luminous faces and ground beams following its actual driving matrix. KayKit windows are marked from small vertical faces using the diagonal-reflection atlas tile (u<0.245, v>0.755), then randomly enabled on about 78% of panes by building instance. Blue paint is never used as a glass classifier. Other assets require an explicitly named glass/window material; unsupported palette buildings remain unlit until the V7 replacement. Up-facing roof faces and broad walls cannot light; the former world-space sparkle grid is removed.

Primary implementation references: [N8AO usage and performance](https://github.com/N8python/n8ao), [Three SMAAPass](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/jsm/postprocessing/SMAAPass.js), [Three OutputPass](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/jsm/postprocessing/OutputPass.js). Installed sources were checked for colour-space and disposal behavior. N8AO 2.0.1 has no complete dispose override, so its owned render targets, quads and materials are explicitly released when size or quality changes.

Actual checks, rejected images and performance belong in PLAN.md and evidence/visual/v3. These rendering changes do not finish V4 furniture staging or V5 crowds.

The accepted32room views run at59.73FPS or better. Laptop Cozy pixel cost has median4.49ms and max6.02ms, above the approximate3–4ms target in some views; Low has median2.55ms. Quality-toggle checks show stable GPU geometry/texture counts. Timer diagnostics are development-only. Entering a room cancels any outstanding outdoor camera flight.


## V4 rooms and staged activities
The original character GLBs are unchanged. CC0 Quaternius Universal Animation Libraries 1 and 2 are retargeted at 24 Hz onto both shipped 62-bone bodies by scripts/retarget-activities.mjs. Compressed character rest transforms are recovered from inverse bind matrices in the production Three loader. Retargeted Sit, Lie, Sleep, Type, Eat, Dance, Cheer, Jog and Talk use the existing single AnimationMixer and 0.2-second cross-fade. All ten wardrobe rigs share compatible bind transforms (maximum measured matrix difference 0.0000155).

shared/interiors.json owns city-specific furniture, walk-up stations, collision boxes and hip/facing slots. The server validates the approach and reserves a free slot, sends activitySlot in sim/presence/position frames and clears it at completion. The visual character walks into the slot without moving the authoritative approach position. Indoor actions last six seconds, leaving time for staging before the server settles money and needs. Park posture has a separate /api/posture endpoint, validates bench/proximity/occupancy, grants no money or XP and clears when walking resumes.

All four 3 m walls are built. Settings offers Walls Up, Cutaway (camera-facing upper walls hidden) and Walls Down (0.25 m remaining wall). Metre-scale chairs, desks, beds and tables follow the shared layout. Sleep fits under a raised blanket; the shower has frosted glass. Owned tiling floor and wallpaper textures, skirting, ceiling lamps, plants, shelves and city-specific clutter add room detail. Static furniture/floor contact is baked once; geometry, textures and wall resources are released when a room changes. Visual evidence and actual checks are indexed in evidence/visual/v4; V8 is the final three-city sweep.

V3 audit follow-ups in V4 also smooth coincident cloth normals in owned runtime geometry (cached GLBs stay unchanged), give each room a supported ceiling diffuser and darken its surrounding garden. All sampled GPU timings and limits remain in evidence; native resolution is never lowered.
